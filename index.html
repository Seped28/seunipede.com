I'm a geospatial guru and environmental scientist dedicated to leveraging spatial technology and AI to solve complex global challenges. I bridge data-driven innovation and community wellbeing, with a track record spanning academic research, sustainability advocacy, and applied product building.

My work explores environmental monitoring, geomatics, spatial epidemiology, and the built environment's effect on human health, using remote sensing, GIS, and geographically weighted models (GWR / MGWR / GTWR) to reveal patterns that global, non-spatial statistics miss.

Beyond the lab, I'm a Surveying professional, an FAA Certified Remote Pilot, and the product owner of YASA Nigeria a university discovery & AI counseling platform for Nigerian students.

02 — Research

& In Progress

My research applies spatial statistics and remote sensing to public health, climate resilience, and urban planning. Full record on Google Scholar ↗ and ResearchGate ↗.

2026

Urban Oases: The Critical Role of Green and Blue Spaces in Mental Well-Being

Ipede, O.; Lin, M.; Hladik, C.; Tu, W. — Sustainability, 18(2), 642.

A national-scale study across major U.S. cities (2015–2017) examining how urban green and blue space exposure relates to Frequent Mental Distress. OLS regression found statistically significant but spatially uniform associations, while Geographically Weighted Regression (GWR) revealed considerable spatial variation in effects across cities, evidence that "one-size-fits-all" urban planning misses local nuance.

DOI: 10.3390/su18020642 ↗ Georgia Southern Digital Commons ↗
2026

Land Use/Land Cover Change Detection and Assessment of Flood Susceptibility in the Niger Delta Region

Tosin-Orimolade, A.; Rahman, M.K.; Ipede, O. et al. — Climate.

Investigates how intensive oil exploration and gas flaring in the Niger Delta drive local climate change and land cover shifts that compound flood risk — a region where flooding remains a recurrent disaster disproportionately affecting vulnerable communities.

DOI: 10.3390/cli14050108 ↗
2025

Urban Oases: The Critical Role of Green and Blue Spaces in Mental Wellbeing

Presented at the American Association of Geographers (AAG) Annual Meeting — Detroit, MI.

Presented findings to a global audience of geographers, GIS scientists, and public health experts, described by Ipede as "the Super Bowl of spatial science." The talk argued that good city planning isn't just about infrastructure, it's about healthier, happier people.

Urban Oases II — MGWR/GTWR Extension

Census-tract level, 2015–2023, national coverage.

Extending the original Urban Oases model into a continuous longitudinal Multiscale Geographically Weighted Regression (MGWR) and Geographically & Temporally Weighted Regression (GTWR) study, with detailed case studies across Atlanta/Fulton County, Miami-Dade, and Los Angeles.
